Emergency Preparedness & Power Outage Buying Guide

Be Ready Before the Power Goes Out

Storms and unexpected outages can interrupt lighting, cooking, communications, and access to essentials. A practical emergency kit can make these situations easier to manage.

Start with safe lighting, drinking water, shelf-stable food, medications, a first-aid kit, and a way to receive weather alerts.

1. Emergency Candles — 50 Pack

Emergency candles can provide backup lighting when electricity is unavailable. They are best treated as a secondary option because open flames introduce fire risks.

Safety: Never leave candles unattended or burn them while sleeping. Keep them away from children, pets, and flammable materials. Battery-powered lights are generally a safer first choice.

2. Portable Wood-Burning Camping Stove

A portable wood-burning camping stove may be useful for outdoor cooking when conventional cooking equipment is unavailable, provided it is operated according to its manufacturer's instructions.

Safety: Never operate a fuel-burning stove inside a home, garage, vehicle, or other enclosed space. Carbon monoxide can be deadly. Use only in approved locations with the required ventilation and fire precautions. Never assume a tent is suitable unless the manufacturer expressly approves the setup.

3. Other Essentials Worth Having

  • Battery-powered flashlights and spare batteries
  • Power banks for charging phones
  • Drinking water and nonperishable food
  • First-aid supplies and essential medications
  • Battery-powered or hand-crank weather radio
  • Warm blankets and weather-appropriate clothing

Check your supplies regularly, and tailor your kit to your household's medical, accessibility, and pet needs.
