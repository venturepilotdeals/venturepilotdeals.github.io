How to Choose an Air Fryer for Your Kitchen

Air fryers use circulating hot air to cook food. Choosing the right one depends on your household size, available counter space, and cooking habits.

1. Choose the Right Capacity

Smaller baskets can work well for one or two people. Larger households may prefer a bigger basket or a dual-basket design. Compare usable cooking space rather than relying only on advertised capacity.

2. Compare Basket and Oven-Style Models

Basket-style air fryers are often convenient for smaller portions. Oven-style models may accommodate different food shapes and cooking accessories but can take up more space.

3. Look at Cooking Controls

Consider adjustable temperature settings, timers, and presets. Presets can be convenient, but accurate controls and straightforward operation matter more than the number of buttons.

4. Think About Cleaning

Check whether removable parts are dishwasher-safe according to the manufacturer. Look for accessible surfaces and consider how easy it is to remove grease and crumbs.

5. Consider Safety and Placement

Follow the manufacturer's clearance instructions, use a heat-resistant surface, and avoid blocking ventilation. Check the power requirements and safety instructions before use.

6. Compare Total Value

Compare warranty coverage, replacement parts, basket size, ease of cleaning, and return policies. Verify current prices and availability directly with the retailer.

Air Fryer Buying Checklist

  • Will the cooking capacity suit my household?
  • Will it fit safely on my counter?
  • Are the controls easy to use?
  • Are the cooking parts easy to clean?
  • Does the warranty meet my needs?
